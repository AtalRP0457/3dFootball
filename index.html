    How to play
    
        
    
          
          
          
          
          
          
        
    

        
    
          
    Move and sprint
    
          
    
            
    P1 WASD move   Left Shift sprint
    
            
    P2 ↑←↓→ move   Right Shift sprint
    
            
    Pad left stick or D-pad   RT / RB sprint
    
          
    
          
      
            
    • You control the player with the green ring and the arrow over his head.
      • 
            
    • Up on the keyboard runs towards the far touchline, right runs towards the right-hand goal.
      • 
            
    • Sprinting uses stamina (green bar, bottom of the screen). Let it refill by jogging.
      • 
            
    • Running with the ball is a little slower than running without it.
      • 
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    Pass
    
          
    
            
    P1 F or Space
    
            
    P2 . (period)
    
            
    Pad A / ×
    
          
    
          
      
            
    • The teammate with the yellow ring is who the pass will go to. Your name panel shows it too ("Next").
      • 
            
    • Hold a direction to choose: the yellow ring moves to the teammate in that direction. Tap Pass when the right player is yellow.
      • 
            
    • After you pass, you take control of the receiver (he turns green) and the passer goes back to the computer.
      • 
            
    • Don't touch the keys and your receiver runs onto the ball by himself.
      • 
            
    • A tap is a ground pass along the floor. E (P2 \, pad RB+A) plays a harder driven pass, R a through ball into space, V a lofted pass (a cross from the wing into the box). Only taps longer than about 40 m go in the air on their own.
      • 
            
    • Press pass or shoot while the ball is still on its way to your player and he plays it first time.
      • 
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    Change player
    
          
    
            
    Without the ball, press Pass: P1 F   P2 .   Pad A
    
          
    
          
      
            
    • When the other team has the ball, Pass switches you to the yellow player: your teammate nearest the ball.
      • 
            
    • Control also changes on its own when:
              
        
                
      • you pass (you get the receiver),
        • 
                
      • a computer teammate wins the ball (you get him),
        • 
                
      • the opponent wins the ball and another teammate is clearly closer.
        • 
              
      
            
      • 
            
    • Red rings are players the computer controls. You never control the goalkeeper; he plays by himself.
      • 
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    Shoot
    
          
    
            
    P1 hold G   P2 hold /   Pad hold B / ○
    
          
    
          
      
            
    1. Hold Shoot: the red power bar fills up.
      2. 
            
    2. While holding, press up to aim at the far post or down for the near post.
      3. 
            
    3. Release to shoot.
      4. 
          
    
          
      
            
    • A quick tap is a low driven shot. About half power is a normal, accurate shot. Full power is a power shot: very fast but it can fly over the bar.
      • 
            
    • Curl (P1 H, P2 ', Pad LB+B) bends a side-foot finish into the far corner. Chip (T, ], LT+B) lifts it over a keeper off his line.
      • 
            
    • Shooting at a full sprint, across your body or with the ball away from your feet costs accuracy.
      • 
            
    • Players with better shooting ratings miss less.
      • 
            
    • Best from inside or near the penalty box. Fast shots are harder for the keeper to catch.
      • 
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    Defend and tackle
    
          
    
            
    Without the ball, press Shoot to tackle: P1 G   P2 /   Pad B
    
          
    
          
      
            
    • Get close (about one step) to the player with the ball, then tackle.
      • 
            
    • Good defenders win more tackles. A missed tackle slows you down for a moment.
      • 
            
    • Press Pass to switch to the teammate nearest the ball when your player is too far away.
      • 
            
    • Your computer teammates press and cover too, so you don't have to chase everything.
      • 
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    Rules and screen
    
          
      
            
    • Kick-off, throw-in: the taker can't run. Choose a direction and press Pass. Shooting a throw-in just throws it.
      • 
            
    • Corner: press Pass for a pass, or Shoot to whip it towards goal.
      • 
            
    • Goal kick: your keeper takes it automatically.
      • 
            
    • Choose 4-3-3, 4-4-2 or 3-5-2 in Team Management. Offside is called when a player beyond both the ball and second-last defender receives a pass.
      • 
            
    • Late tackles can be called as fouls; fouls in the penalty area award a penalty kick.
      • 
            
    • Hard fouls can injure a player. Injured players lose pace and accuracy; use Team Management to replace them.
      • 
            
    • Pause to open Team Management. Set the support tactic and make up to three substitutions; a change queued during play takes effect at the next stoppage.
      • 
            
    • The teams change ends at halftime. Sound can be toggled from the match HUD.
      • 
            
    • Esc or P pauses · C changes the camera · R skips a goal replay.
