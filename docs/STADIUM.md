# Stadium, pitch presentation and pre-match intro

## Files

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `src/render/stadium/layout.json` | **The single source of truth** for the stadium layout: pitch size, stand offsets, terrace rows/rise/depth, aisles, roof, tunnel, dugouts, screens. Used by both the Blender build and the game. |
| `tools/blender/build_stadium.py` | Blender (Python) script that models the stadium from the layout, saves the editable `.blend` and exports the GLB. |
| `assets-src/blender/stadium.blend` | **Editable Blender source** (generated by the script; open it in Blender 4.x/5.x). |
| `public/assets/stadium/stadium.glb` | Runtime model loaded by the game (~1.7 MB, ~15k faces, no textures). |
| `src/render/stadium/stadium.js` | Loads the GLB (`loadModel`), keeps the procedural stadium as the fallback, seats the instanced crowd on the modelled terraces, drives the big screens and LED ribbons, floodlight glow, quality levels. |
| `src/render/stadium/pitch.js` | Pitch, grass shader and markings (unchanged geometry: markings are real geometry, aligned to the gameplay coordinates). |
| `src/render/scene.js` | Loads the stadium, graphics quality (`setGraphics`), pre-match intro director (`playIntro` / `skipIntro`). |

## What the model contains

Named objects, merged per material (few draw calls):

- **Stands** – stepped lower tiers on all sides and corners, upper tiers on the far side, both ends and far corners; pitch-side walls.
- **Seats** – seat pans and backs per row, in blocks between aisles (blue, with white accent rows). The crowd (instanced in the game) sits on the same rows.
- **Aisles** – lighter stair strips with half steps every 12 m, with handrails.
- **Vomitories** – entry tunnels cut into the lower tiers (dark openings, parapets, front rails).
- **Concourse** – concourse floor and back wall between the tiers with lit doorways, the upper-tier fascia and soffit.
- **Roof / RoofGlass** – cantilever trusses every 12 m, columns, metal deck with standing-seam ribs, a glazed leading edge, closed far corners.
- **Lamps / Masts** – floodlight banks under the roof girders and two corner masts (emissive lamp faces; the game adds glow sprites at their positions).
- **Facade** – cladding and vertical fins behind the stands (the TV side is kept low so the broadcast camera can see over it).
- **Tunnel** – the players' tunnel at the halfway line on the near side (the near LED boards leave a gap for it).
- **Dugouts** – two benches with glass roofs.
- **Screen_A / Screen_B** – giant screens over the far corners (live canvas in game: teams, score, minute, stadium name).
- **LED_Ribbon_*** – ribbons on the upper-tier fascias (scrolling sponsor boards in game).
- **Ground** – the ground outside the bowl.

All sponsors and names are fictional. Club names and the stadium name shown on
screens and captions come from the game's own database.

## Rebuilding / editing

```bash
npm run stadium:build
```

(runs `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup --python tools/blender/build_stadium.py`;
on other systems run the same with your `blender` executable). Change dimensions in
`layout.json`, rebuild, reload the game: crowd seats follow automatically.

To edit by hand, open `assets-src/blender/stadium.blend` in Blender and export
with *File → Export → glTF 2.0 (.glb)*, selection = all mesh objects, +Y up, to
`public/assets/stadium/stadium.glb`. Keep the object names listed above (the
game looks up `Screen_A`, `Screen_B`, `LED_Ribbon_*`, `Lamps`, `Masts` by name).
Note: re-running the script overwrites the `.blend`.

If the GLB is missing or fails to load, the game logs a warning and keeps the
procedural stadium, so it always starts.

## Quality levels (Settings → Match settings → Graphics quality)

| | High | Medium | Low |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pixel ratio cap | 2 | 1.5 | 1 |
| Sun shadow map | 3072 | 2048 | 1024 (hard PCF) |
| Crowd drawn | 100 % (~24.9k) | 75 % | 45 % |
| Floodlight glow | on | on | off |

## Pre-match intro (Settings → Pre-match intro: On/Off)

~15 s, played once per fixture (a rematch of the same teams goes straight to
kick-off; never in practice, drills, penalties or indoor games):
establishing orbit → glide down to the tunnel → teams walking out → dolly along
the faces → boots and kit → line-up with the match caption → sweep from the
side to the gameplay camera. It uses the same stadium and the same player
models as the match. **Enter / Space / Escape / click / pad A or Start skip
it** immediately; the match (clock, ball, positions) only starts once it ends
or is skipped, and the skip press is not passed on to the match.
